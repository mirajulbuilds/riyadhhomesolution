# Riyadh Home Solution — AI Image Prompts

All site images are AI-generated for now. To keep every image looking like one set, **always paste the STYLE BLOCK first, then the subject line**. Save each image with the file name shown (it matches the service slug), export as JPG/PNG, and upload through the admin panel — the panel converts it to WebP.

Good tools: Midjourney, Ideogram, Leonardo, Google ImageFX/Gemini, ChatGPT images. Generate 4 variations, pick the cleanest.

---

## STYLE BLOCK (paste before every subject)

```
Photorealistic professional photo, modern Saudi home interior in Riyadh, clean and bright natural daylight with soft warm ceiling light, neutral beige and white walls, tidy and premium but realistic. Technician wears a navy blue work uniform with small orange accents, black work gloves, no visible logos or text on clothing. Face not the focus — hands and the work are the focus, or the technician is shown from the side/back. Shallow depth of field, 35mm lens, eye-level, sharp details on the tools and fittings. Colour grade: cool navy shadows, warm whites, a touch of orange. No text, no watermark, no brand names, no logos on products.
```
